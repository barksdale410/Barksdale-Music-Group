🎵 BARKSDALE MUSIC GROUP

**The most comprehensive AI music and movie creation platform.**

Barksdale Music Group is your complete AI‑powered music and movie production ecosystem — from beat making to distribution, all from your iPhone.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Add your chord progression** to `chords/progression.txt`
2. **Push to GitHub** → GitHub Actions generates MIDI automatically
3. **Download** the MIDI files from Actions
4. **Import to GarageBand** and start producing
